## Stage Transition Criteria
- Client expresses readiness to move on
- Client has achieved the core objectives of the current stage
- Client shows signs of impatience or desire to progress faster
- The conversation has naturally evolved beyond the current stage focus
- No critical missing criteria remain
- Any remaining missing criteria are non‑critical and can be deferred
