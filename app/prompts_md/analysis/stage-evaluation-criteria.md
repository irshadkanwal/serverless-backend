## Stage Evaluation Criteria
- Has the purpose of this stage been fulfilled?
- Has the client demonstrated understanding of key concepts from this stage?
- Is there evidence the client is ready to progress?
- Has the client directly or indirectly expressed a desire to move forward?
- Would continuing in this stage add significant value, or would it be repetitive?
- Are there unresolved missing criteria relevant to the current stage?
- Are any critical inputs still absent, preventing goal validation for this stage?
- Do any missing criteria signal the need to loop back or delay transition?
- Have all critical missing criteria been addressed or explicitly deferred?
- Are any non‑critical missing criteria present that do not block progression?
- Have there been 3+ failed attempts to satisfy critical criteria?
- Does the conversation show 5+ cycles without meaningful progress?
- Are there signs of client confusion or frustration with clarifications?
- Do responses indicate gaps in foundational understanding from earlier stages?
