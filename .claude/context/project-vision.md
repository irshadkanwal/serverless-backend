# Project Vision

## Vision Statement
To democratize access to professional coaching by creating an AI-powered platform that delivers personalized, ethical, and transformative coaching experiences at scale, making professional growth and development accessible to everyone, everywhere, at any time.

## Mission
CoachBot empowers individuals and organizations with intelligent, compassionate, and structured coaching support that adapts to unique needs, maintains professional standards, and drives meaningful personal and professional growth.
